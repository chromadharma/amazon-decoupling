The pipeline

Nine stages, and why they are kept apart

Every byte enters through one fetch function that retries, then stamps the URL, a UTC timestamp, the size and a SHA-256 hash into a provenance manifest. Nothing is estimated straight from an API. Each stage writes a file the next stage reads, so any one of them can be re-run alone.

StageWhat happens
1 FetchRetry, then hash and log to data/raw/_manifest.jsonl. 207 entries.
2 MastersThe 772-municipality list, the geometry, the biome overlay. Built first; everything joins to it.
3 AcquireOne script per source, each writing a tidy interim table.
4 AttributeThe spatial work: deforestation polygons and forest-loss pixels assigned to municipalities.
5 PanelAssemble the municipality × year grid, deflate, derive rates and growth.
6 AuditEight checks on identifiers, coverage and provenance. A non-zero exit blocks a rebuild.
7 EstimateThe method ladder, in order, writing to results/.
8 CasesDossiers for municipalities the panel selected, not chosen in advance.
9 FiguresRead only from results/, never from a scrollback.

The separation is not tidiness. Three of the data-integrity bugs found in this build were invisible in the code and visible only in a written file — a column of fabricated zeroes, two files with the same hash, and an input set that was never recorded. If estimation had read straight from the source, none of them could have been caught after the fact.

Joins

Every join, and what it costs

A join is the assertion that two datasets are about the same thing, which makes it the most dangerous step in any build. Each one below has a key, a failure mode, and where possible a measured error rate.

Deforestation polygons → municipality

This is the join the literature does not disclose. The official product ships state and satellite path, but no municipality field. Every municipal deforestation figure published in this field is therefore somebody's spatial join, made with somebody's boundary vintage and somebody's rule for polygons that straddle a border, and none of them say which.

0.41% is the number to quote. It is what it costs to assign a straddling polygon whole rather than splitting it. It is small — and it is disclosed and measured, which is the point.

Alerts → municipality

Trivial, and worth saying so: the alert product ships a municipality code. Zero of 457,815 alerts carry a code outside the master list and none are null. No name matching, no spatial work, no error. When a source does the join for you, say that it did.

Forest-loss pixels → municipality

Municipal polygons are rasterised onto the Hansen grid, so a boundary resolves at 30 metres and the straddling problem does not arise. Pixel area is computed analytically per raster row from its latitude (see formulas §8) rather than by reprojecting, because the loss year is a class code and resampling it would invent years that were never recorded.

The hardest join here, because the archive publishes PDFs carrying municipality names and no codes.

The parser that produced those rows is validated at 91% against the reports' own printed totals across 159 state-years. Three failure modes bit at least once. A conflict listed as spanning three municipalities becomes three rows, and counting it whole in each gives a regional total of 11,740 land conflicts against 8,732 apportioned fractionally, a spill ratio of 1.34 — both series are carried. Splitting names on the word "e" would break Pontes e Lacerda, so the splitter uses punctuation only. And an alias table entry pointing at a municipality that does not exist cost 68 rows before the audit caught it.

Decisions

Choices that could have gone otherwise

The universe is the agency's own list, not a hand-built one

Building the municipality list by hand from "the nine states" gets Maranhão wrong, because only the part west of the forty-fourth meridian is inside. The agency publishes its own share-inside figure for every municipality, which a hand-built list does not have and which turns out to matter enormously.

Two calendars, never merged silently

The deforestation year runs 1 August to 31 July; the statistical year is the calendar year. About 58% of a deforestation year falls in the year it is labelled with. The panel carries both alignments and refuses to pick one, because several of the hypothesised lags are one to three years and the misalignment is worth five months of that.

Public administration netted out of the economy

A municipality whose GDP rises because federal transfers rose has more value allocated to it, not more value produced in it. One specification therefore reports output net of public administration, and it is the one where the growth result actually clears significance: +4.15 with p = 0.014, against +2.20 and p = 0.060 for the headline. This is a theoretical distinction — use value against exchange value — implemented as a single subtraction, and it is the clearest example in the build of theory doing work inside the pipeline.

Structural zeroes excluded, not zeroed

133 of the 772 municipalities record no deforestation in any year, and their mean Amazônia-biome share is 0.16. They sit outside the forest mask. Leaving them in with a value of zero estimates an average over a population containing cases where the outcome cannot occur, and attenuates every coefficient. The modelling sample is the 546 municipalities with at least 5% biome. Open question: 546 with the biome cut, or all 772 with a biome-share control. It affects every coefficient.

A zero only where the instrument was looking

The rule that fixed two of the three data-integrity bugs. A municipality-year gets a zero only if its source published that year. The deforestation product publishes from 2008; the conflict archive covers 17 of 23 years. Outside those windows the value is missing, not zero, and the windows are derived from the data rather than hard-coded so they stay correct when the sources extend.

Deflation by a national index, with a known bias

There is no municipal deflator. In frontier municipalities land, freight and cattle prices move differently from the national consumer basket, so every real series carries a known bias of unknown sign. Reported, not hidden.

Where the qualitative meets the quantitative

Four places a technical choice is a substantive one

The methodological claim this project makes is not that numbers illustrate experience. It is that every measurement choice already contains a claim about what kind of thing is being measured, and that making those claims explicit is what turns critical political economy from a vocabulary into a method.

The 6.25-hectare threshold is a class filter. A family clearing two hectares is not recorded as deforestation; a rancher clearing two hundred is. It is a deliberate engineering choice to control false positives, and its consequence is that the official record of forest loss is systematically better at seeing large actors than small ones. Every statistic built on it inherits that.

A conflict count is a duration, not an intensity. The archive re-registers a continuing situation each year it stays unresolved, so eighteen years of one invasion and eighteen separate incursions score identically. For the people living it those are completely different conditions. Measured across the region, the median Roraima municipality holds 58% of its conflict record in a single case against 5–16% elsewhere — see the maps page.

Fixed effects deliberately discard the most dramatic comparison. Removing everything permanent about a place is what makes the estimate credible, and it means the design throws away the contrast between an Indigenous territory in Roraima and a soy municipality in Mato Grosso, which is the comparison a reader most wants explained. The panel can only learn from change within a place over time. That is not a limitation to apologise for; it is the division of labour that justifies reading the cases separately.

A denominator decides what "the place" is. Dividing by the whole municipal area for a Maranhão municipality that is 4.7% inside the Legal Amazon asserts that the other 95.3% is part of the Amazon story. It is not. Choosing the effective in-boundary area is a claim about the object of study, disguised as arithmetic — and getting it wrong understated a rate twenty-one-fold.

Limits

What this design cannot do

Stated plainly, before anyone has to extract it

Nothing here is causal. The design shows that in the years a municipality cleared more, it also recorded more agricultural value, more land conflict and more concentrated farmland than the year effect predicts. Anything that varies within a municipality over time and moves both sides at once escapes it.

There is no data on who benefits. "Concentrated" is measured. "A handful of people" is an inference no series here counts — nothing in this build observes an owner, a firm, or a household.

The welfare window ends in 2010. The human development series is published for census years, so the properly powered null is a statement about the 2000s, not about the period the argument is mostly about.

The conflict archive counts what was reported. Municipality fixed effects absorb the permanent reporting density of a place, but not changes in reporting effort over time.

The radar workstream is withdrawn. It was not reproducible; see formulas §9.

The one design in the plan that could support the word caused is a natural experiment on protected areas that were legally shrunk on a known date, comparing clearing in the excised portion against the retained portion of the same reserve. It is specified and not built.
